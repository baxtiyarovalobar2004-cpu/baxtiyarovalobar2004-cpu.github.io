I'm Lobar Bakhtiyarova (Lobar Baxtiyarova), an SMM strategist and content creator based in Urgench, Khorezm region, Uzbekistan. I run several brand accounts at once: private medical clinics, a premium dental clinic, an academic lyceum and education centers.

I measure the work in numbers, not likes: how many leads came in, what each lead cost, how many patients or students signed up. Strategies are built on the SOSTAC framework, competitor ads are reviewed in the Meta Ad Library, and owners get a clear monthly report.

I'm currently building my own SMM agency with a small team of videographers and a graphic designer. I also teach English.
